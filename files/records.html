Payout days

Records are grouped by the payday that pays them. Set the days you get paid (for example 5 and 20). If your payroll closes a few days before payday, enter those days as the cutoff. Day 31 uses the last day of shorter months.

Deductions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG)

Employee shares only: SSS 5% of the monthly salary credit (₱5,000 to ₱35,000), PhilHealth 2.5% of basic pay (₱10,000 to ₱100,000), Pag-IBIG 2% of up to ₱10,000 (max ₱200). Income tax is not included.
