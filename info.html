What is WageCal?

WageCal is a free salary calculator for Philippine workers who are paid by the day. Log the hours you work, and it works out your pay the way DOLE rules describe: overtime, rest days, holidays and night differential. It then subtracts your SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions so you can see your take-home pay.

Your entries are saved to your own account, so you can open WageCal on any phone or computer and find your records waiting.

How to use it

  1. Log in or sign up with your email and a password of at least 6 characters.
  2. Set your daily wage rate. On the Calculator page, pick your province and sector and the minimum wage fills in. Or type your own rate if you are paid more than the minimum.
  3. Choose night differential. Leave it on if you work between 10PM and 6AM. The default is +10%.
  4. Add a work day. Choose the date, time in, time out and your unpaid break. Set the day type (regular or rest day) and any holiday. Tick "Did not work that day" for an absence. The preview shows your pay before you save.
  5. Tap "Save to my records". A popup confirms the save, and the date moves to the next day so you can keep logging.
  6. Set your payout days. Enter the days you get paid, for example 5 and 20. Add cutoff days if your payroll closes a few days before payday.
  7. Set your deductions. Choose which of SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG apply to you, what they are based on, and which payday they come out of.
  8. Open the Records page. See your pay for every payday, every month, and each saved day, with gross pay, deductions and net salary. Tables show 10 rows per page.
  9. Delete a mistake. Tap ✕ on an entry, or use "Clear this month". You will be asked to confirm first.

On a phone, tap the calculator icon at the top right to open the menu.

How your pay is computed

The daily rate is divided by 8 for the hourly rate. The first 8 hours (after your unpaid break) are regular hours, and anything beyond that is overtime.

Night differential: hours worked between 10PM and 6AM earn an extra 10% of the hourly rate for that hour. Absent on a regular holiday: you are still paid 100% of the daily rate.

Provincial minimum wage

Minimum wages are set by region, and some areas have their own rates. Choosing a province fills in the reference rate for that area and your work date, so a wage increase applies from its effective date. The rates are a guide compiled from public NWPC summaries and news reports, and some sources disagree. Always confirm the rate with your wage order or employer, and type your own rate if it differs.

Payout days

Records are grouped by the payday that pays them. With paydays on the 5th and 20th, work from Sep 21 to Oct 5 is paid on Oct 5, and work from Oct 6 to Oct 20 is paid on Oct 20. If your payroll closes earlier than payday, set the cutoff days. A payout day of 31 uses the last day of shorter months.

Contributions are worked out from your gross pay for the payout month (or from your daily rate × 26 days if you choose that option), then taken out on both paydays or on the one you pick. Net salary is gross pay minus these three. Income tax and other deductions such as loans are not included.

Good to know

Is this my official payslip?

No. WageCal gives an estimate. Your employer's payroll is the final word on your pay.

Can I log a shift that goes past midnight?

Yes. Set the time out earlier than the time in, for example 22:00 to 06:00, and WageCal counts it as one overnight shift.

Why does my entry use the old rate after the wage changed?

Each saved day keeps the rate it was saved with. Change the rate before you log new days.

Where is my data stored?

In your own account, protected by your login. Other users cannot see your records.
